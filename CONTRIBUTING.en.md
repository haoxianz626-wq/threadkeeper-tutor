# Contributing to ThreadKeeper Tutor

[English](CONTRIBUTING.en.md) | [简体中文](CONTRIBUTING.md)

Thank you for helping people learn without losing their place.

1. Open an Issue describing the problem, expected result, and steps to reproduce. **Never include private learning histories or personal data.**
2. Changes to `SKILL.md` must be evaluated against both [Chinese tests](tests/scenarios.md) and [English tests](tests/scenarios.en.md).
3. Keep the Chinese and English command maps consistent, including reading status, assessment, and checkpoint semantics. Translation must not turn an unread lesson into a mastered one.
4. Favor small, accessible explanations and low cognitive load over more features, more content, or longer responses.
5. Pull requests should describe what changed, how it was tested, and potential compatibility effects on existing checkpoints.
6. Do not add intrusive device monitoring, shame-based motivation, or storage that leaks real learner data.

By contributing, you agree to license your contribution under the project's [MIT License](LICENSE).
